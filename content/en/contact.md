There are two good ways to reach me, depending on how urgent it is.

### The hotline (fast)

The [hotline](/#hotline) on the homepage is for a specific question or a short call. One question, two lines, a real human voice on the other end — no bot triage, no ticket number. If you're trying to figure out whether I'm the right person for your agentic-AI problem, start here.

### Email (slower, more depth)

For anything that needs context — a project, a longer question, something you want me to read carefully — email is the better channel:

- **alex@girard-davila.net**

I read every message personally. Expect a reply from a human, usually within a couple of working days.

### What to include

A few lines on what you're building, what's breaking (or what you're worried might break), and what "good" looks like for you. The more concrete the question, the more useful my first answer.

- **Objectives**: map the company's workflows, score what can be
  automated **now** with agent tools (and what cannot), quantify the
  gain per workflow, and produce a prioritized roadmap leadership can
  execute without me.
- **Audience**: executives + 3 to 6 key people; the audit is done
  **inside** the company, on its working data (anonymized if needed).
- **Prerequisites**: access to 2 to 3 people per service involved for
  1-hour interviews.
- **Deliverables / measurable outcomes**:
  - a **workflow map** (10–25 workflows covered, maturity score 0–4 for
    each);
  - a **prioritized roadmap**: top 5 automations with estimated gain
    (hours/week) and risk, sequenced over 6 months;
  - a stack recommendation (local vs. hybrid) with
    cost/risk justification — written to be read by the executive
    committee.

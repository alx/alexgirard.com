### Typical combinations

| Situation | Path |
|---|---|
| Solo decision maker | M1 (½ day) → decision → M5 if "yes" |
| Tech team | M2 + M3 (3 days) → M4 optional |
| Mixed team (5–8 people) | M1 (½ day) then M2–M5 over 5 days |
| NGO / association | M1 + M2, 100% local stack |

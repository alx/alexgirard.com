- **Objectives**: understand what an AI agent actually does (plan, call
  tools, iterate until validated) and where it fails; distinguish
  chatbot, assisted tool and autonomous agent; choose the right level of
  automation for a given use case.
- **Audience**: managers, project leads, decision makers; no technical
  prerequisites (demos are live, not video).
- **Prerequisites**: a laptop for the hands-on part (30 min of guided
  practice).
- **Deliverables / measurable outcomes**:
  - every participant leaves with **3 candidate automations** identified
    in their own workflows (use-case sheet provided);
  - short comprehension test (10-question quiz) — target ≥ 8/10;
  - an argued decision: "let's talk it over" vs. "let's move to M2/M3".
